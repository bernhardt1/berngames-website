Controls

Tap a barrel to ignite. Hold briefly to preview its direct blast, then release to cancel safely. Drag one tile into an adjacent empty space. Clear every rival in one chain.

Keyboard: Tab to a barrel, Enter to ignite, hold Space to preview, arrows to move. Z undoes; R restarts. Settings contains sound controls, instructions and hints.

Made with AI-assisted development

Designed and playtested by Bern Games with AI-assisted code and text, AI-generated character/prop artwork, and AI-generated instrumental music. The soundtrack is darker Goblin Caper.
