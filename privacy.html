Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers the Bern Games website, its browser games, Hero King marketing pages, and native Pincer Patrol. Other native apps have their own in-game privacy notices.

What we collect

Boom Brigade on berngames.com counts visits, play sessions, attempted boards and verified clears. An anonymous first-party cookie recognizes a browser for up to six months; it is not connected to your email. These statistics do not follow you across other websites. We do not use advertising pixels. Aggregate counts are public; individual gameplay records are not.

Browser game saves

Pincer Patrol, Boom Brigade and Bag of Trouble keep progress and preferences in your browser's local storage. On berngames.com, Boom Brigade also sends accepted puzzle actions to verify clear statistics; these actions contain no free-form text or email address. Anonymous browser identity is approximate: other devices or cleared cookies count separately. Clearing browser data removes local saves. Saves on other game-hosting sites are separate.

Release updates

If you opt in, we store your email address, selected game interests and consent date privately in our Cloudflare database. We use them to tell you about new BernGames releases. Signup is optional. You can unsubscribe using the link shown after signup and included in release emails, or contact us to remove your information. We do not sell this list.

Optional game purchases

Pincer Patrol offers optional Crosswind and Stoneguard Packs. Its ten-board campaign and hints remain free. A separate player profile connects purchases across browsers when you restore with your private recovery code. Your browser or app stores that code; our Cloudflare service stores its hash, a random player ID and profile creation time. Purchase access is checked against provider records. Puzzle moves and progress stay on your device. Anyone with your recovery code can use the profile, so keep it private.

RevenueCat manages purchase records and access checks. Web checkout is provided by Stripe Managed Payments and Link. They collect payment and billing details, including your email, billing location and payment method, to process payments, calculate tax, provide receipts, prevent fraud and support refunds. BernGames does not receive your full card number. See RevenueCat privacy and Link privacy. These purchase details do not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Contact us about purchase-access or profile-data requests. Payment records may need to be retained by the payment providers. Browser releases currently show no ads.

Native Pincer Patrol

The first iOS version offers ten free boards and two separate permanent packs, Crosswind and Stoneguard. Progress and sound preferences stay on your device. The purchase profile uses the private recovery-code system described above. Production and testing services keep separate profiles.

Native iOS purchases use Apple's billing with RevenueCat to check ownership, support restores and handle purchase records. RevenueCat also provides purchase reporting to Bern Games. The game does not receive your Apple password or full payment credentials. This iOS version contains no advertising SDK, shows no ads and does not use advertising identifiers or advertising pixels. Android and earlier rewarded-ad experiments remain separate from this version.

Our Cloudflare commerce service uses hour-specific hashes of request IP addresses and expiring request counts to limit abuse. Expired counts are removed during subsequent request handling. Standard hosting request metadata may also be processed for security and performance. Purchase records are held by the store and RevenueCat; their retention can differ from local game data.

For profile-access, deletion or other privacy requests, contact us below. Removing the app clears its local progress; it does not automatically delete provider payment records or the server profile.

Earlier private rewarded-ad tests

Earlier private test builds used optional Google AdMob ads and its consent service. Those services may process IP address and approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions, crash information and performance data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See Google privacy and Google's SDK data disclosure. Where those builds provide ads, requests wait until the consent service permits them and the pack menu provides available ad privacy choices.

For those tests, Google sends our Cloudflare service a signed ad-completion record containing the game profile ID, a one-use verification code and ad transaction details. We retain verification and daily-access records to prevent repeated rewards. The limit is one bonus board per profile per UTC day. Test ads do not generate advertising revenue. Free boards and hints do not require watching an ad. The rewarded-ad SDK and ad requests are absent from the first iOS version described above.

How we use information

Email messages are used to respond to your request, provide support, or coordinate testing opportunities. We do not sell or share personal information for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Standard server logs may record basic request metadata for security and performance.
