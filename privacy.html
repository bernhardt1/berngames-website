Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy covers the Bern Games website, its browser games, Hero King marketing pages, and native Pincer Patrol testing. Other native apps have their own in-game privacy notices.

What we collect

BernGames browser games count visits, play sessions, attempted boards and verified clears. An anonymous first-party cookie recognizes a browser for up to six months; it is not connected to your email. These statistics do not follow you across other websites. We do not use advertising pixels. Aggregate counts are public; individual gameplay records are not.

Browser game saves

Boom Brigade saves your current board, completed boards, best move counts, and sound preference in your browser's local storage. The game does not upload your save. On berngames.com it sends accepted puzzle actions to verify clear statistics; these actions contain no free-form text or email address. Anonymous browser identity is approximate: other devices or cleared cookies count separately. Clearing browser data removes them. Saves on other game-hosting sites are separate.

Release updates

If you opt in, we store your email address, selected game interests and consent date privately in our Cloudflare database. We use them to tell you about new BernGames releases. Signup is optional. You can unsubscribe using the link shown after signup and included in release emails, or contact us to remove your information. We do not sell this list.

Optional game purchases

Pincer Patrol offers an optional Crosswind Pack. Its ten-board campaign and hints remain free. A separate player profile connects purchases across browsers when you restore with your private recovery code. Your browser stores that code; our Cloudflare service stores its hash, a player ID and purchase-access records. Puzzle moves and progress remain in your browser. Anyone with your recovery code can use the profile, so keep it private.

RevenueCat manages purchase records and access checks. Web checkout is provided by Stripe Managed Payments and Link. They collect payment and billing details, including your email, billing location and payment method, to process payments, calculate tax, provide receipts, prevent fraud and support refunds. BernGames does not receive your full card number. See RevenueCat privacy and Link privacy. These purchase details do not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Contact us about purchase-access or profile-data requests. Payment records may need to be retained by the payment providers. Browser releases currently show no ads.

Native Pincer Patrol testing

Pincer Patrol's iOS and Android versions are in development. Puzzle progress stays on your device. The optional purchase profile uses the recovery-code system described above. Native purchases, when available, use Apple or Google billing with RevenueCat; the game does not receive your full payment credentials.

Rewarded ads are optional. Google AdMob and its consent service may process IP address and approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions, crash information and performance data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See Google privacy and Google's SDK data disclosure. The current iOS test build does not request Apple's tracking permission or access the advertising identifier through that permission.

Ad requests wait until the consent service permits them. Use Ad privacy choices in the Crosswind Pack menu to review available privacy options. Google sends our Cloudflare service a signed ad-completion record containing your game profile ID, a one-use verification code and ad transaction details. We store verification and daily-access records to prevent repeated rewards. The reward limit is one bonus board per profile per UTC day. Test ads do not generate advertising revenue. Free boards and hints do not require watching an ad.

For profile-access, deletion or other privacy requests, contact us below. Removing the app clears its local progress; it does not automatically delete provider payment records or the server profile.

How we use information

Email messages are used to respond to your request, provide support, or coordinate testing opportunities. We do not sell or share personal information for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Standard server logs may record basic request metadata for security and performance.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email berngameshq@gmail.com.
